iSMS GW Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026 · Applies to iSMS GW for Android, version 1.0 and later

iSMS GW ("the app") is an Android SMS app with a built-in SMS & USSD gateway, developed by iSmartyPro. This policy explains what data the app processes and how. In short: the app has no cloud service and never sends any data to the developer.

1. Data processed

  • SMS messages — text, sender/recipient number, time and delivery status, to display conversations and run the gateway.
  • SIM card details — carrier, SIM phone number, slot and subscription id, to route messages through the right SIM.
  • USSD requests and responses and a call log (number, time, duration) when you use these features.
  • Technical log — gateway events and IP addresses of local-network clients, for security (firewall, brute-force protection).

The app does not collect location, contacts or advertising identifiers and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Storage

Data is stored only on your device: in the Android system SMS store and in the app's private database. Web-panel passwords and API keys are stored as cryptographic hashes; tokens and secrets are encrypted with an Android Keystore key. History is deleted automatically according to the retention period you choose.

3. Sharing

The developer receives no data. Data leaves the device only according to your own configuration:

  • to the webhook URLs you add;
  • to your SMS server (WebSocket) whose address and token you enter;
  • to your Telegram bot, if you enable it and link a chat — message texts and numbers then pass through Telegram's servers and are processed under Telegram's privacy policy;
  • to clients that call the local API with an API key you issued, and to the web panel after signing in.

The gateway is off until you start it. HTTPS and WSS encrypt data in transit; plain HTTP on the local network does not — enable HTTPS in the settings.

4. Permissions

  • SMS (send, receive, read) — core function of a default SMS app;
  • Phone — identify SIM cards for dual-SIM support, USSD requests and click-to-call;
  • Notifications — new-message and gateway status notifications;
  • Network — local server, webhooks and the connection to your server.

5. Your control and deletion

You can clear the gateway history and logs in the web panel, export or delete your data with the backup feature, and remove all app data by uninstalling the app. SMS in the system store are managed like any other Android messages.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Политика конфиденциальности iSMS GW

iSMS GW — SMS-приложение для Android со встроенным SMS- и USSD-шлюзом. У приложения нет облака, рекламы и аналитики, и оно не передаёт данные разработчику. Обрабатываются SMS (текст, номер, время, статус), сведения о SIM-картах, USSD-запросы, журнал звонков и технический журнал безопасности. Данные хранятся только на устройстве; пароли и ключи — в виде хешей, токены и секреты зашифрованы ключом Android Keystore. Данные покидают устройство только по вашей настройке: на ваши вебхуки, ваш SMS-сервер, ваш Telegram-бот (через серверы Telegram) и клиентам с выданным вами API-ключом. Историю можно очистить в веб-панели; удаление приложения удаляет все его данные. Контакты: www.ismarty.pro.
